How to play
  1. Drag anywhere to aim. Tap the scope to zoom in; slide the zoom bar for more or less magnification.
  2. Tap the red button to fire. Bolt-action rifles need a moment between shots. Automatics fire as long as you hold the button.
  3. You carry one weapon of each class you own: sniper, automatic, pistol and rocket launcher. Switch with the weapon bar under the item buttons. A reload keeps going after you switch away.
  4. Rockets never run out, but each load takes a while. A rocket that lands next to your own wall damages it.
  5. Headshots deal triple damage and pay 50% more. Soldiers' helmets stop the first shot to the top of the head, so aim for the face.
  6. Shoot bloaters before they reach the wall. Their blast hurts anything close by.
  7. In rescue missions, survivors have a green marker. Don't hit them.

No sound? Flip the ring/silent switch on the side of the iPhone; Safari mutes game audio in silent mode.

Play offline on iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Launch it once while online; after that it runs without a connection.
